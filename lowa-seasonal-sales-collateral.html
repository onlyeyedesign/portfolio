Selected work  /  LOWA

Seasonal Sales
Collateral

More than 30 seasonal workbooks, catalogs and product highlight brochures, adapted from LOWA’s European brand designs for U.S. print and digital sales materials since 2019.

ClientLOWA
My roleDesign · Production
Year2019–2026
ToolsInDesign · Photoshop · Illustrator · Acrobat · Excel · Claude
ResultEvery season since 2019, and no book has missed a sales meeting
01 / Context

The challenge

LOWA’s U.S. sales team needed accurate, current workbooks, catalogs and product highlight brochures for dealer meetings each spring/summer and fall/winter selling season. The larger books were generally adapted from European A4 designs to U.S. formats.

Product imagery, specifications and final line details often arrived close to deadlines. The pages had to remain consistent and production-ready as content changed.

02 / Contribution

Systems that hold
up under deadlines.

I adapted LOWA’s European book concepts for U.S. print and digital editions, built repeatable template pages and styles, and managed production work as product information and imagery changed.

01 / ADAPTATION

European to U.S. format

Rebuilt designs for U.S. page sizes. For earlier catalogs of up to 120 pages, I also designed category openings and coordinated additional design support on tight schedules.

02 / PAGE SYSTEM

Templates and styles

Created reusable template pages and style sheets to keep product imagery, specifications and technology icons consistent throughout each book. A line-list spreadsheet helped organize product details as content changed.

03 / PRODUCTION

Color, print and digital

Retouched and color-corrected product imagery, supported copy updates, attended press checks over many seasons, and published digital editions to LOWA’s dealer and professional sites, updating them as products changed.

03 / 2027 Edition

A refreshed direction,
adapted for the U.S.

For 2027, I adapted LOWA’s refreshed brand direction from an incomplete A4 concept into a U.S. portrait workbook. The Outdoor Collection edition is 56 pages, part of a shift over the years from larger catalogs to more focused workbooks.

I also built an icon-selection sheet with Claude, organizing 60 technology and fit icons in LOWA’s global order. Product-category tabs and fixed columns were designed to make manual icon selection more consistent.

04 / Outcome

Ready for every
sales meeting.

Since 2019, I have prepared the seasonal workbooks and catalogs for the Outdoor Collection and the Cold Weather, Hunting, Professional (Task Force) and Trail Running (All Terrain Running) lines, in print and digital, alongside product highlight brochures. None of the books missed a sales meeting.

The work reinforced the value of consistent templates, reliable product data and clear production processes. Next, I’d connect approved line-list data directly to the InDesign templates, cutting repetitive entry and the errors that come with it.
